# NuroTok Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 October 2026.

## Who we are
NuroTok is an Android and iOS sensory regulation and digital wellbeing app provided by NeuroDigital Support Ltd. Contact: info@neurodigitalsupport.com; +44 7825 354088; https://neurodigitalsupport.com. This policy covers the app and related support. Features vary by platform and version.

## Information on your device
Profiles, preferences, selected apps and schedules help apply your chosen protections. Local insights include times, categories, service or surface identifiers and counts of filtering, blocking and temporary unlocks. Wellbeing records can include completed sessions, favourites, programme and challenge progress, reminders, moods and journal text. Self-check history stores a summary score, number answered and time, rather than a saved list of individual answers. This information can reveal sensitive details about your wellbeing. It is not automatically uploaded as account history.

On supported Android devices, screen filtering processes visible frames and recognised content locally to decide whether to cover content. Frames are analysed in memory and are not saved or uploaded by the filtering feature. A photo selected for a filter preview is also analysed locally. Installed-app visibility and usage access help list selectable apps and identify the foreground app. These are not used for advertising profiling. On iOS, Screen Time frameworks use app selections and activity schedules for supported restrictions; capabilities differ from Android screen filtering.

## Optional accounts
Where enabled, you may sign in using email and password, Google or Apple. Firebase Authentication processes account identifiers, email, authentication credentials and provider information; a display name may be supplied by the provider. Authentication services also process technical connection information for operation and security. An account can link supported premium entitlements across platforms. Signing in does not automatically synchronise your journal, practice history, profiles or settings.

## Purchases
Apple or Google processes store payments. RevenueCat validates purchases and manages premium access using transaction, product, entitlement, subscription and renewal information, an anonymous app-user identifier or linked account identifier, and technical SDK information. NuroTok does not receive full payment-card details. Premium access can work without a NuroTok sign-in where supported.

## Analytics and crash reports
Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics are enabled in NuroTok. They can process app interactions, event parameters, crash logs, error context, stack traces, installation or app-instance identifiers, device and operating-system details and connection metadata. Examples include app opens, filtering use, blocked service categories and unlock duration. These reports are not intended to contain screen frames, recognised screen text, journal entries or chat messages. Identifiers can distinguish installations, so these reports should not be treated as necessarily anonymous.

Usage statistics and Crash reports are enabled by default. Change them under You > Privacy. Turning collection off stops future collection through those settings but does not erase information already transmitted. Contact us about previously collected information. These settings do not replace any separate consent required by applicable law.

## Notifications
Local reminders use your chosen schedule. When you allow notifications, Firebase Cloud Messaging processes a registration token and technical delivery information for remote announcements. Change announcement preferences where available or revoke notification permission in device settings. Notifications and analytics have separate controls.

## AI conversation and speech
AI chat shows a consent notice for each conversation. Sending a message transmits recent conversation turns and app instructions to Groq and Google Gemini. A failed attempt can cause the same conversation to be sent again to the other provider. NuroTok does not automatically attach your mood history, journal, profiles or filter history. Any information you type into chat is part of the information you choose to send. Providers also receive network metadata such as IP addresses.

Messages stay in the current screen's memory rather than a saved NuroTok chat history; closing the screen ends the local conversation. This does not delete information already sent to a provider. Provider logging, safety review, retention and model-improvement practices are governed by each provider's terms and account settings. Avoid identifying, medical or confidential information.

Microphone access is used when you start dictation. Recognition prefers supported on-device processing. If cloud recognition is needed and you permit it, the system speech provider receives speech for transcription; on many Android devices this is Google. NuroTok does not deliberately save an audio recording. The device's speech service has its own privacy terms. Sending a transcript to chat is a separate action.

## Backups, websites, downloads and support
Export creates a readable JSON backup of available local sections, including settings, profiles, meditation history and insights. This export is not encrypted by NuroTok. A temporary copy is written to app cache; destinations selected in the share sheet can receive it. Imported files are read to restore backup data. Protect and remove exported copies yourself, including cloud or messaging copies.

Lite mode loads third-party websites in a WebView. Sites receive visits and information you submit and may use cookies, sign-in sessions, analytics and advertising under their own policies. Shared cookies are enabled in the reviewed WebView. Changing or hiding a feed does not prevent the website from collecting information. Other browsers or apps opened by links have their own privacy practices.

Configured rule, model or version downloads expose ordinary technical request information to hosting services; these requests are separate from frame analysis. Support messages give us your email, message, attachments and any information you include.

The NeuroDigital Support website uses Google Analytics to measure website visits and usage only if you accept analytics in Cookie settings. The analytics tag is not loaded before consent. You can reject or withdraw consent at any time using Cookie settings in the website footer.

## Purposes and legal grounds
We process information to deliver requested features, authenticate users, validate purchases, provide support, secure services and understand usage or reliability where enabled. Where UK or EEA law applies, relevant grounds may include contract for requested account and purchase services, consent where required for optional processing, legitimate interests in security and handling enquiries balanced against your rights, and legal obligations for required records. Device permission is not a substitute for legally required consent. Sending sensitive information to an online feature may require explicit consent where applicable. You may withdraw consent for future processing without affecting processing already lawfully carried out.

## Recipients and international processing
Depending on your choices and features used, recipients include Google/Firebase, Apple, RevenueCat, Groq, system speech providers, hosting and support providers, Google Analytics for the website where you consent, and sharing destinations you select. Third-party websites control their own services. Information may also be disclosed when required by law, necessary to protect legal rights and safety, or during a business transfer subject to applicable safeguards. NuroTok does not sell personal information or use it for our own cross-service targeted advertising.

Online providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Where data protection law requires safeguards for an international transfer, the relevant provider and NeuroDigital Support Ltd. use a lawful transfer mechanism applicable to that processing. Contact us for further information about the safeguards applicable to your request.

## Retention and deletion
Local records remain until you delete them, overwrite them with a backup restore, clear app storage or otherwise lose the stored data. Some lists have rolling limits: journal, completion and assessment histories each retain up to 2,000 entries. Frames are transient; chat has no saved in-app history. Exported backups are independent copies.

Account records are kept until you request deletion, subject to provider deletion processes and any records that must be retained for legal obligations, disputes or fraud prevention. Deleting sign-in does not automatically remove RevenueCat, telemetry, store or support records. Financial records are retained only for as long as needed for legal, accounting or fraud-prevention purposes. Analytics, crash-report, push-token and AI provider records follow the applicable provider's retention terms and account settings; third-party sites and stores control their own records. Contact us for information about the retention period applicable to a particular request.

Use You > Account > Delete account to remove sign-in. Use You > Privacy > Delete all data for local information; Delete insights removes the insights history. Account deletion and local deletion are separate. Without the app, email info@neurodigitalsupport.com with subject “NuroTok account/data deletion”. We may require proportionate verification, never your password. See the Account and Data Deletion page. Deleting an account, data or the app does not cancel store subscriptions.

## Rights and choices
You can edit preferences, remove journal entries where offered, export data, change sharing choices and revoke device permissions. Depending on your location, rights may include access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection, portability, withdrawal of consent and a complaint to a supervisory authority. Contact us to exercise applicable rights. We cannot remotely retrieve local-only records, but can explain local controls. We respond within applicable legal deadlines. UK users may contact https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/; users elsewhere can contact their local regulator.

## Children, security and changes
NuroTok is intended for adults aged 18 and over. It is not designed for children. If you believe a person under 18 has provided personal information, contact us so we can review the request and take appropriate action.

Protect your device, account and backups. No system is completely secure; local app storage and JSON backups should not be assumed to have additional app-level encryption. We may update this policy as features and practices change, giving additional notice or obtaining consent where required. The effective date identifies the published version.
