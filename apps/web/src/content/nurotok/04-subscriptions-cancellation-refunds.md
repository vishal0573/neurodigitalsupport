# NuroTok Subscription, Cancellation and Refund Policy

Effective date: 27 October 2026.

## Checkout and renewal
NuroTok may offer free features and monthly or annual Premium plans. Availability varies by region, platform and build. The store checkout gives the actual price, currency, billing interval, included access, trial conditions and applicable taxes. Only the offer shown to you applies. A trial, introductory offer or earlier-user entitlement is available only if you qualify under that offer.

Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled through the purchasing store before its deadline. Trials convert to the displayed paid plan unless cancelled in time. Check the exact next billing date and any earlier cancellation deadline in your store account. Trial duration and eligibility are shown before purchase. Store rules govern upgrades, downgrades and billing. Price changes follow required store and legal notice and consent processes.

## Cancellation
Use You > Account > Manage or cancel. Apple: https://apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions. Google Play: https://play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions. Sign in with the purchasing store account, select NuroTok and follow the cancellation steps. Save confirmation. If no subscription appears, check which account made the purchase.

Cancellation normally stops future renewal and preserves access until the paid period ends, subject to store rules and applicable law. Trials may end differently. Removing the app, clearing data, signing out or deleting a NuroTok account does not cancel subscriptions.

## Refunds
Apple purchase refund requests: https://reportaproblem.apple.com/; help: https://support.apple.com/billing. Google Play refund policy and request guidance: https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637. The store assesses requests under its rules and applicable law. Contact info@neurodigitalsupport.com for help identifying a charge or resolving an issue. Do not send full payment details.

Cancellation does not automatically refund earlier charges. Nothing here removes mandatory refund, withdrawal or digital-content remedies under applicable consumer law. We do not impose an absolute “no refunds” rule.

## Restore access and avoid duplicate billing
Use You > Account > Restore purchases with the original store account. Where enabled, optional NuroTok sign-in can link supported entitlements across Android and iOS. Billing remains with the purchasing store; linking does not transfer or cancel a subscription. Check existing access before buying again on another platform. Restoring purchases does not synchronise local journals, settings or histories.
