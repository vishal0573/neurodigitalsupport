# NuroTok Wellbeing and AI Disclaimer

Effective date: 27 October 2026.

## General wellbeing
NuroTok provides sensory regulation and digital wellbeing tools, including meditation, breathing, journaling and self-checks where available. It is not a medical device or a service for diagnosis, treatment or prevention of illness. Self-check scores cannot establish or rule out a diagnosis. Do not change prescribed treatment or delay professional care because of app content.

## AI limitations and emergencies
Optional AI conversation is automated. It is not therapy, counselling or a conversation with a healthcare professional. Replies can be wrong, incomplete or unsuitable. NuroTok cannot assess your full circumstances. No person monitors conversations in real time on your behalf. Automated crisis screening can miss danger and does not create an emergency-response service. If there is immediate danger, contact local emergency services or seek immediate help from a trusted person nearby. Do not rely on this app for emergency decisions.

Review each chat consent notice. Recent conversation turns can go to Groq and/or Google Gemini, including fallback attempts. The app has no saved chat history after the conversation screen closes, but providers may retain information under their terms and settings. Avoid identifying or confidential information. Dictation may use a cloud speech service if separately allowed. See the Privacy Policy.

## Exercise and sensory suitability
Choose settings and sessions appropriate for you. Stop if audio, breathing, visual effects, movement or filtering causes dizziness, distress or discomfort. Obtain qualified advice about suitability where you have a relevant condition. Keep audio at comfortable levels. Do not perform exercises requiring attention or closed eyes while driving or operating machinery.

## Protection limits
Filters may miss unwanted material or cover harmless content. Device and website changes affect results, and Android and iOS features differ. NuroTok does not guarantee a particular wellbeing outcome or a completely safe browsing environment. These statements do not exclude mandatory consumer rights or liability that cannot lawfully be limited.
