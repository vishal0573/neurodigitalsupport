# NuroTok Support and Contact

Effective date: 27 October 2026.

## Contact
NeuroDigital Support Ltd.: info@neurodigitalsupport.com; +44 7825 354088; https://neurodigitalsupport.com.

## Reporting issues
Include Android or iOS, app version, device model, a brief description and reproduction steps. For purchases, include the store and an order reference if needed. Redact private details from screenshots. Do not send passwords, verification codes, full card information or unnecessary journal or health information. Support correspondence is processed to answer your request; see the Privacy Policy.

## Common requests
For billing, use You > Account > Manage or cancel. Restore purchases uses the original store account. Privacy choices and local deletion are under You > Privacy. Sign-in deletion is under You > Account > Delete account. Without the app, email us with subject “NuroTok account/data deletion”. Local deletion, account deletion and subscription cancellation are separate operations. See the dedicated pages for details.

## Accessibility, safety and security feedback
Report inaccessible controls, filtering errors, distressing content or security issues to the same email. Describe the issue without sending sensitive material unless necessary and requested. Support is not an emergency or clinical service and has no promised live monitoring. For immediate danger, contact local emergency services.
