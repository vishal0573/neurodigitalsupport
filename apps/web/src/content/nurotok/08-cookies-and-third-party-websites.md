# NuroTok Cookies and Third-Party Websites Notice

Effective date: 27 October 2026.

## Lite websites
Lite mode opens third-party websites in an embedded WebView and may alter which interface elements appear. Those services can receive your IP address, device or browser details, visits, interactions, sign-in details and anything submitted. They can use cookies or similar storage for login, preferences, analytics or advertising under their own policies. The reviewed WebView enables shared cookies; sessions can persist or be shared where supported. NuroTok does not promise anonymous browsing or prevention of third-party tracking.

Use each service's privacy and cookie controls. Sign out when appropriate and use available website, browser or device controls to manage site storage. Deleting NuroTok profiles does not delete third-party accounts and should not be assumed to clear every WebView cookie. Links opened in another app or browser use that service's settings. App preference storage is different from browser cookies.

## NeuroDigital Support website
NeuroDigital Support Ltd. uses Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use neurodigitalsupport.com. The Google Analytics tag is not loaded unless you choose “Accept analytics” in the cookie banner. If you reject analytics, the tag remains disabled. Your choice is saved in browser local storage under neurodigital-website-cookie-consent for up to 180 days; this preference storage is used only to remember your choice. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time using “Cookie settings” in the website footer.

After you accept analytics, Google Analytics may set the _ga and _ga_GSXRNMBYNX cookies to distinguish browsers and sessions and measure site usage. Their default duration can be up to two years, subject to browser and service settings. Google receives usage and technical information associated with website visits. See Google's privacy information at https://policies.google.com/privacy. The site does not load the analytics tag before consent. If you clear browser storage or cookies, the site may ask for your choice again.

This notice describes the analytics technology identified on the website. Hosting and security systems may also process technical request information to deliver and protect the site. Cookie settings for neurodigitalsupport.com do not control cookies or similar storage used by third-party websites opened in the NuroTok app.
